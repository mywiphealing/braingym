myWIPhealing for Education

Creative mental health programmes for schools & universities

Students are carrying more than grades: pressure to perform, social media, stigma, and not knowing where to turn. We bring art therapy, psychoeducation and peer support onto campus, from primary pupils to foundation-year students, and show you what changed.

Art therapy sessions

Guided workshops on emotional regulation, mindfulness, body acceptance and self-worth. Visual art, music, movement and drama. No artistic skill needed.

🤝

Peer-supporter training

Our SHIPS programme trains students and staff to run art-based peer support, so care continues after we leave.

🌱

Campus outreach days

Awareness events and panels in the spirit of our Art of Healing festival, built around your students' wellbeing, inclusion or exam season.

Activities used

Every campus report includes

  • Anonymised before-and-after wellbeing scores for each session
  • Themes students raise, so counsellors know where to focus
  • Attendance and engagement across the term
  • Student reflections, shared only with their consent
  • A printable one-page summary for your management or board
