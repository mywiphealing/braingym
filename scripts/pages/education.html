What we bring

Creative wellbeing, built for campus life.

Art therapy sessions

Guided workshops on emotional regulation, mindfulness, body acceptance and self-worth, through visual art, music, movement and drama.

Peer-supporter training

Our SHIPS programme trains students and staff to run art-based peer support, so care continues after we leave.

Campus outreach days

Awareness events and panels in the spirit of our Art of Healing festival, built around your students' wellbeing, inclusion or exam season.
